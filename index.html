Overview

Through this historic commitment, the University has advanced initiatives that strengthen student success, expand scholarship opportunities, enhance campus planning, and position the institution for long-term sustainability and growth.

This report covers the accomplishments achieved through the partnership between Huston-Tillotson University and The Moody Foundation during 2025–2026. Highlights include:

  • 01Execution of the grant agreement
  • 02Establishment of a $10 million UNCF scholarship endowment
  • 03Engagement of CPM Texas as the University's owner's representative
  • 04Appointment of key leadership to support implementation of the gift
  • 05Completion of the University's branding and capital campaign framework with Deutser
  • 06The presentation of RamChella 2026
  • 07Strategic planning for the “For Such a Time as This” capital campaign
  • 08Receipt of the first grant installment
  • 09Coordination of the gift receipt with JPMorgan Chase
  • 10Development of the Moody Final Mile Scholarship Program
  • 11Recognition of Ross and Elle Moody at the 2026 Commencement Ceremony

Collectively, these accomplishments demonstrate measurable progress toward transforming the student experience while laying the foundation for future campus expansion, philanthropic growth, and institutional excellence.

Mission and Vision

Mission and Vision

Mission

Huston-Tillotson University is a faith-based historically black college and university (HBCU) nestled in the vibrant heart of downtown Austin, Texas. The institution provides a robust liberal arts and STEM education through market-driven certifications, associate, baccalaureate, master’s degrees, and centers of excellence. HT remains committed to its legacy of educating a mosaic community of learners while cultivating innovative leaders who are dedicated to academic excellence and service.

Vision

Huston-Tillotson University commits to cultivating the forerunners of innovation. We aspire to prepare graduates who are pioneers, socially engaged leaders and thinkers, demonstrated through academic excellence and service.

The Work

Four bodies of work carried the year.

  • CPM Texas engaged as Owner's Representative and program management firm, providing oversight, strategic planning, and project management for campus expansion.
  • Attorney Jeffrey S. Howard of McLean & Howard, LLP retained as land use counsel for zoning, permitting, and regulatory matters.
  • A comprehensive project management structure established to strengthen oversight, with a predictable framework for professional services and financial management across the project lifecycle.
  • City of Austin approvals ahead for the first phase: HT Student Apartments and the Arena.

  • A $10,000,000 endowed scholarship fund created with UNCF, expanding scholarship opportunity and reducing financial barriers for future students.
  • The Moody Final Mile Scholarship, designed to support eligible undergraduate seniors in their final year, removing financial barriers that would otherwise delay graduation.
  • Faculty, staff, and students convened through focus groups in July 2026 to finalize program logistics and policy.
  • RamChella enhanced student engagement across spiritual reflection, academic readiness, cultural programming, and entertainment.

  • Comprehensive branding initiative and capital campaign brand development completed with Deutser on May 27, 2026.
  • Refreshed the University's overall brand identity, evolving HT's visual and messaging foundation to reflect its 150-year legacy and vision for the future.
  • Capital campaign theme established: “For Such a Time as This.”
  • Introduced “We Are the Soul of Success,” the new university brand campaign uniting HT's story under a shared idea of purpose-driven achievement.
  • Attorney Delphia York Ridley, who has served as Associate Director of External Affairs at the Smithsonian National Museum of African American History & Culture, invited to advise the campaign process.
  • The HOMECOMING documentary was presented at the Martha's Vineyard African American Film Festival as the first launch of national storytelling in support of the campaign.

  • Dr. Dandreon C. Gray, Major Gifts Manager, funded with University operational dollars, manages the Moody gift and reports directly to President Wallace on all aspects of it.
  • Canary Dennis, Director of Sponsored Programs and Title III, provides grant administration, building project liaison, reporting, and compliance support.
  • Receipt and endowed acceptance of the gift were coordinated with JPMorgan Chase and Moody Foundation representatives. The donation is accruing interest as intended, with a report from the JPMorgan Chase investment team among the referenced exhibits.
