Dr. Melva K. Wallace

Overview

Through this historic commitment, the University has advanced initiatives that strengthen student success, expand scholarship opportunities, enhance campus planning, and position the institution for long-term sustainability and growth.

This report covers the accomplishments achieved through the partnership between Huston-Tillotson University and The Moody Foundation during 2025–2026. Highlights include execution of the grant agreement, establishment of a $10 million UNCF scholarship endowment, engagement of CPM Texas as the University's owner's representative, appointment of key leadership to support implementation of the gift, completion of the University's branding and capital campaign framework with Deutser, the presentation of RamChella 2026, strategic planning for the “For Such a Time as This” capital campaign, receipt of the first grant installment, coordination of the gift receipt with JPMorgan Chase, development of the Ross Moody Finishing the Last Mile Scholarship Program, and the recognition of Ross and Elle Moody at the 2026 Commencement Ceremony.

Collectively, these accomplishments demonstrate measurable progress toward transforming the student experience while laying the foundation for future campus expansion, philanthropic growth, and institutional excellence.

Mission

Huston-Tillotson University is a faith-based Historically Black College and University in the heart of Austin, Texas, providing robust liberal arts and STEM education through market-driven certifications, associate, bachelor's and master's degrees, and centers of excellence.

Vision

To cultivate the forerunners of innovation by preparing graduates who become pioneers, socially engaged leaders, and critical thinkers through academic excellence and service.

The Work

Four bodies of work carried the year.

  • CPM Texas engaged as Owner's Representative and program management firm, providing oversight, strategic planning, and project management for campus expansion.
  • Attorney Jeffrey S. Howard of McLean & Howard, LLP retained as land use counsel for zoning, permitting, and regulatory matters.
  • A comprehensive project management structure established to strengthen oversight, with a predictable framework for professional services and financial management across the project lifecycle.
  • City of Austin approvals ahead for the first phase: HT Student Apartments and the Arena.

  • A $10,000,000 endowed scholarship fund created with UNCF, expanding scholarship opportunity and reducing financial barriers for future students.
  • The Ross Moody Finishing the Last Mile Scholarship designed to support eligible undergraduate seniors in their final year, removing financial barriers that would otherwise delay graduation.
  • Faculty, staff, and students convened through focus groups in July 2026 to finalize program logistics and policy.
  • RamChella enhanced student engagement across spiritual reflection, academic readiness, cultural programming, and entertainment.

  • Comprehensive branding initiative and capital campaign brand development completed with Deutser on May 27, 2026.
  • Campaign theme established: “For Such a Time as This.”
  • Attorney Delphia York Ridley, who has served as Associate Director of External Affairs at the Smithsonian National Museum of African American History & Culture, invited to advise the campaign process.
  • The HOMECOMING documentary was presented at the Martha's Vineyard African American Film Festival as the first launch of national storytelling in support of the campaign.

  • Dr. Dandreon C. Gray, Major Gifts Manager, funded with University operational dollars, manages the Moody gift and reports directly to President Wallace on all aspects of it.
  • Canary Dennis, Director of Sponsored Programs and Title III, provides grant administration, building project liaison, reporting, and compliance support.
  • Receipt and endowed acceptance of the gift were coordinated with JPMorgan Chase and Moody Foundation representatives. The donation is accruing interest as intended, with a report from the JP Morgan investment team attached.
  • The owner's representative agreement remains in legal review to secure proper protections for the University.
